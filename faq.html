Foundation & Cracks

About Your Foundation

The most common questions we get about foundation cracks, causes, and repair options.

01 How do I know if my foundation has a serious crack?
Not all cracks are equal. Hairline cracks under 3mm are usually cosmetic, but horizontal cracks, stair-step cracks on brick walls, or any crack wider than 5mm can indicate structural movement or water intrusion. If you see water stains, mold, or doors that suddenly stick, book a professional inspection.
02 What causes foundation cracks in the first place?
Foundation cracks are typically caused by: soil movement from wet/dry cycles, hydrostatic water pressure pushing against basement walls, poor drainage around the home, tree roots drawing moisture from the soil, and natural settling of a new home over the first 5–10 years.
03 How long does foundation crack repair take?
Most single-crack repairs are completed in 2–4 hours. If you have multiple cracks or a full waterproofing job, expect 1–3 days. Epoxy injections cure within 24 hours; polyurethane injections cure in about 4 hours. You can typically use your basement normally the same day.
04 Will repairing a crack fix my water leak permanently?
If the crack is the actual source of the leak — yes. But water often finds multiple entry points. That's why we always inspect the entire basement and foundation before repairing. A full assessment ensures your solution isn't just a temporary patch.
05 Is epoxy or polyurethane injection better for my crack?
It depends on the goal. Epoxy is best for structural cracks because it restores strength and bonds the concrete back together. Polyurethane is best for leaks because it expands to fill voids and stays flexible. For most cracks we use a combination — epoxy for structure, polyurethane for sealing.
06 Can I repair foundation cracks myself?
For very small cosmetic cracks, DIY filler can work temporarily. But structural cracks, horizontal cracks, and any crack that leaks should be handled by a professional. Improper repair can hide the problem, worsen water damage, and void your homeowner's insurance.
07 Is foundation crack repair covered by warranty?
With DryShield — absolutely. Every crack repair we do comes with a written, transferable warranty. If the same crack leaks again, we return and fix it at no cost. Our standard warranty covers structural integrity for 10 years and water-tightness for 5 years.
Sump Pumps & Waterproofing

About Your Basement

Everything you need to know about sump pumps, waterproofing, and keeping water out for good.

Do I really need a sump pump if my basement has never flooded?
Yes — a sump pump is preventative. Basements can flood suddenly after heavy rain, snowmelt, or plumbing failures. It's the single best protection you can have, even if you've never had water.
What size sump pump do I need for my basement?
Most homes do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower pump. Larger basements, high water tables, or frequent storms need a 3/4 HP unit. We size yours precisely during your free inspection.
Do I need a battery backup for my sump pump?
Absolutely — power outages often happen during the worst storms, exactly when you need the pump most. A battery backup keeps your system running for up to 7 hours. It's cheap insurance against thousands in damage.
How often should a sump pump be serviced?
Once a year, ideally before storm season. We clean the pit, test the float switch, check the discharge line, and inspect the check valve. An annual service extends pump life by 3–5 years.
What's the difference between interior and exterior waterproofing?
Interior waterproofing manages water once it's inside — drainage channels, sump pumps, and vapor barriers. Exterior waterproofing stops water before it enters — membranes and drainage outside the foundation. The best solution often combines both.
How long does a full basement waterproofing job take?
Most residential basement waterproofing projects take 2–4 days. Larger basements or full exterior excavation can take 5–7 days. We give you a clear timeline after your inspection.
Roughly how much does basement waterproofing cost?
Interior systems typically range from $3,000 to $8,000. Exterior waterproofing can range from $8,000 to $20,000+ depending on size and accessibility. We provide a written, itemized quote — never a surprise bill.
