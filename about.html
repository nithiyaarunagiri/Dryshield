Our Story

From a Small Team to a Trusted Name

DryShield was founded in 2001 with a simple belief: every homeowner deserves a dry, safe basement — no exceptions, no shortcuts. What started as a two-person crew has grown into one of the region's most trusted waterproofing companies.

Over the years, we've protected more than 2,500 homes from water damage. Our approach has never changed: honest assessments, quality craftsmanship, and written guarantees that actually mean something.

Michael Robertson Founder & CEO
